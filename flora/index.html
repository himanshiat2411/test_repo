Liliaceae

Lily

Lilies grow from bulbs that rest underground through winter. In summer a single stem rises, buds swell, and each flower opens one petal at a time.

Origin
Asia, Europe,
North America
Family
Liliaceae

Observe the beauty of the lily family

Liliaceae holds about 15 genera and around 600 species. Most grow from bulbs and carry six petal-like tepals around six stamens.

Growing a lily

Planting
Set bulbs about 15 cm deep in autumn or early spring, in soil that drains well.
Light
Full sun on the flowers, with the roots kept cool and shaded.
Water
Keep the soil evenly moist while the stem grows. Water less once the flowers fade.
Bloom time
Early to late summer, depending on the type. Each flower lasts about a week.

The blue on this page is imagined. Real lilies come in white, pink, red, orange and yellow, but not true blue.
