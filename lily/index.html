What inspired me

A lily I bought, and what it taught me

One day I bought a small bunch of flowers. Inside it was one open lily and a tight green bud. The bud looked like it would never open.

How it shaped me

Watching that bud open, I saw my own story. In chemical engineering I was like that closed bud: growing, but not showing it yet.

The water was the time, the learning and the people who believed in me. Design was the moment I opened.

The lily did not rush, and it did not need anyone to see it grow. When it was ready, it bloomed. That is how I want to grow, and how I want my work to feel.

Like a lily, I took my time.

I started in chemical engineering. For a long time I learned in silence: formulas, labs, late nights. Nobody could see the roots that were growing.

Then I found design, and something opened. Today I try to make work that is calm, honest and useful, and that stays with people after they leave.

I am still growing. But I am no longer hiding underground.
